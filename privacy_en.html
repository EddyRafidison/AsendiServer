OneX Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OneX collects, uses, stores and discloses information when you create an account or use the OneX application and services. It should be read together with the OneX Terms and Conditions.

1. Information We Process

Identity and account information. When you register, we request your full name, date of birth, residential address, declared nationality, declared country of residence, identity-document type (national identity card or passport), identity-document number, email address, account username, password and recovery seed. We also require an image of the identity document for account verification; an additional image may be provided where applicable. The identity-document number is encrypted in the application database. Passwords and recovery seeds are stored as cryptographic hashes rather than as readable credentials. If you live in the country of your nationality, you may submit a national identity card issued by that country; a passport may be used where a national identity card is not available or where it is the identity document you use. If you live in a country other than the country of your nationality, you must submit a valid passport as a foreign resident. This rule applies to all nationalities and does not single out Madagascar or any other country.

Account access, account security and welcome promotion. We process the administrative account-access state, deactivation and reactivation reasons and timestamps, the administrator identifier and account-access change history. Reasons may include suspicious or unusual activity, suspected fraud or abuse, detection of multiple linked accounts or attempts to circumvent service rules, inconsistent signup or identity information, non-compliance with the Terms or other service rules, or a legal or regulatory obligation. To review these situations and prevent multiple accounts, we may compare available account information and reference identifiers, including email addresses, wallet addresses and pseudonymous identity-document fingerprints, within the limits described in this Privacy Policy. We also record the welcome-credit eligibility or decision status, any credited amount, the valuation price used, and decision and credit timestamps. To prevent a second award after account deletion and re-registration, pseudonymous HMAC fingerprints of the email address and identity document are retained in a permanent claim register. The register may also retain the original username, awarded amount and credit timestamp; email addresses and identity-document numbers are not stored there in plain text. These records are used to prevent duplicate awards and maintain an audit trail.

When an account is deactivated, OneX processes the deactivation state, recharge amounts that remain to be credited, new incoming transfers received during deactivation, their hold reasons, associated blockchain events and related notifications. Upon reactivation, the system records the queue wake-up and subsequent automatic processing attempts. These data are retained with the financial history and administrative audit records needed for reconciliation and dispute handling.

Financial and transaction information. Individual remuneration-cycle data is also processed, including reference price A, target B, applied tfees rate, threshold-trigger events, the principal-balance snapshot, the required sale threshold, the frozen XTU-equivalent purchase threshold, cumulative qualifying outgoing P2P sales, cumulative net credited qualifying acquisitions in XTU, the tfees rate and transaction price applied to each operation, progress remaining toward either alternative restart condition, the cycle-close reason and related timestamps. A recharge that remains pending and has not been credited does not trigger a restart. To operate the service, we process account balances and valuation information, transaction fees, recharge and transfer records, seller-program activity, including the main balance used to check the 20 XTU eligibility threshold, committed reserves and gross and net seller payouts, and individual and global disbursement requests addressed to ONEX. For each recharge, we may record the total amount received, associated blockchain events, the tfees rate fixed for the session, gross and net credit tranches, deducted fees, the remaining pending amount, credit sequences and accounting references, hold reasons and timestamps, automatic retry attempts and exceptional refund decisions. These records may be retained while any recharge remainder remains unresolved. When a user requests an exceptional refund review, we also process the request identifier, the user's stated reason, request category and status, the administrative decision and reason, the administrator identifier and related timestamps. For a global disbursement, we also process request status, requested amounts, remaining balances, payment and bonus records, deadlines and administrative-review references. A participant or review record does not itself authorize OneX to debit or pay an amount that the user has not requested. We retain refund records, dates, statuses and references to maintain accounts, process transactions, prevent duplicate payments and resolve disputes. We distinguish the amount remaining to be credited, the amount reserved for a refund and the amount actually refunded. Administrative approval creates a reservation; a refund is counted as completed only after the BSC-USD transfer event is confirmed on-chain. If the transaction is finalized as reverted, the reservation is released and the amount is restored to the pending recharge remainder. Request and refund references, administrative decisions, lifecycle events, estimated and actual fees and transaction hashes are retained in an audit trail. The minimum net amount for a seller payout and an outgoing refund is calculated as twice the maximum BSC gas-fee ceiling configured by OneX. The derived threshold, gross amount, estimated gas and net amount are processed and checked when a payout is queued and again by the final payment worker.

Recharge availability and shared treasury address. To show whether recharge services are available to your account, OneX processes account eligibility and service-status information and retrieves an aggregate XTU availability indicator. The balance endpoint may also return a boolean indicator showing only whether a recharge remains pending, without disclosing its amount. When recharges are open, the application may display OneX's single public treasury address, shared by all users; it is not an individual deposit address. The address may be hidden while recharge services are suspended. Transfers received during an unavailable period may be detected and retained for reconciliation or administrative review. For each recognized incoming transfer after blockchain finality, a notification containing the payment reference, status, current hold reason and suggested next step is placed in the Telegram outbox when the user has linked an active Telegram account. The Telegram worker processes the outbox and retries temporary delivery failures; an account that has not linked Telegram cannot receive Telegram notifications.

Blockchain and wallet information. At signup, each user must provide a fixed public BNB Smart Chain wallet address. OneX validates its EVM address format and uniqueness but does not cryptographically verify that the user owns or controls it. This address is used to identify deposits by matching the sender from address in BEP-20 Transfer events against registered addresses, and it is the destination for refunds and outgoing payouts. OneX processes the token contract, treasury address, sender address, amount, transaction hash, event log index, block number and hash, timestamps and finality status. Transfers whose sender address does not match an account are stored in an unknown-transfer register for administrative decision; they are not automatically credited. A permanent wallet registry retains the public address and its historical account association so that the address is never reassigned, including after account deletion. OneX never asks for a wallet private key or recovery phrase.

Network fee estimates and queued transfers. Before an outgoing BSC-USD payment, refund, seller payment or disbursement, OneX processes the transaction data, estimated gas limit, gas price and BNB/USD quote read from the Chainlink feed on BNB Smart Chain, together with the estimated cost in BSC-USD. If the estimate exceeds the operational limit of 0.4 BSC-USD, the transaction is retained in a queue and re-evaluated later without being signed or broadcast first. Actual network fees, receipts, transaction hashes and the quote used for reconciliation are recorded. Deposits are received directly by the shared treasury; no consolidation from individual deposit addresses is performed.

Support and communication information. If you contact OneX, we process your message and the account and email details needed to respond. If you choose to link Telegram, we store the Telegram user and chat identifiers needed to send account and transaction notifications. Linking Telegram is optional.

Connection and security information. During registration, the server may look up your IP address for approximate country-level geolocation as a technical test. The result, including the country code MG (Madagascar), is not used to determine your nationality, select the identity document you must provide, approve or reject registration, or impose a country-of-residence restriction. The identity-document rule uses the nationality and country of residence that you declare. It does not use the IP geolocation result to infer either value or to select your document. This lookup does not verify your residence. The service also processes security events such as failed authentication attempts, password changes, recovery requests and rate-limit events to protect accounts and prevent abuse.

2. How We Use Information

To restrict an account to one registered Android device, OneX processes the installation identifier, ECDSA P-256 public key and its cryptographic fingerprint, session timestamps, the session-token hash, anti-replay nonces, request signatures, user-agent information and a pseudonymized IP-address fingerprint. The private key remains in Android Keystore on the device and is not collected by OneX. Passwords and account seeds are verified using Argon2 hashes; they are not stored in clear text in device audit logs.

We use the information described above to:

  • create, identify and manage accounts;
  • verify account information and identity documents;
  • authenticate users, support account recovery and protect credentials;
  • maintain account balances and valuations, apply disclosed fees and administer account-level service conditions;
  • identify blockchain deposits and process eligible recharges, P2P transfers, seller payments, user-submitted individual and global disbursement requests, refunds and related administrative reviews;
  • administer a global disbursement notice, request window, user-submitted requests, payment status, unrequested or remaining balances requiring administrative review, and any applicable bonus settlement;
  • send necessary account, security and transaction notifications when a communication channel has been enabled;
  • answer support requests, investigate errors, prevent fraud and maintain the reliability of the service; and
  • meet applicable legal requirements and protect the rights, users and operational security of OneX.

3. Identity Verification and Email Providers

Information and identity-document images submitted during registration may be sent by email to the person or team responsible for reviewing account registrations. Email service providers may process and store those messages and attachments as part of delivering the verification process. Do not submit identity documents belonging to another person unless you are legally authorized to do so.

The signup email sent to the user contains only the username; OneX does not send an email-verification code or link. This notification therefore does not constitute cryptographic proof that the user controls the mailbox. Users must provide an email address they can access and maintain access to it. OneX also uses email delivery for account recovery and support communications. The email provider processes the information required to deliver those messages.

4. Blockchain Processing and Public Information

OneX currently supports Binance-Peg BSC-USD transfers using the BEP-20 token standard on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56). In OneX's operating model, BSC-USD is the stablecoin payment and settlement asset associated with the platform's reserve model; it may also be referred to operationally as USDT BEP-20. XTU is also a separate BEP-20-compatible token on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56), with 18 decimals and a fixed supply of 100 billion tokens minted once to the dedicated distribution wallet. The OneX accounting model uses 1 XTU = 1 BSC-USD as a fixed accounting parity for wallet externalization; it is not a market price feed or a guarantee of redemption. The supported Binance-Peg BSC-USD token is a pegged representation and is not USDt directly issued by Tether. This distinction, and the risks associated with token arrangements, reserves, liquidity and blockchain operation, are described further in the Terms and Conditions. Blockchain transactions are recorded on a public network. Wallet addresses, transaction amounts, transaction hashes and related on-chain information may therefore be visible to the public and cannot be made private or deleted by OneX. Blockchain data may also be processed through the BNB Smart Chain RPC providers used to read or submit transactions.

To manage treasury operations, OneX also processes the public addresses of its internal operational wallets (Hot Wallet, Cold Wallet and dedicated Disbursement Wallet), their on-chain BSC-USD and BNB balances, amounts allocated to uncredited recharges, reserved refunds, unknown incoming transfers held for review, seller payables and signed or submitted but unconfirmed Cold Wallet transfers, together with the configured operating minimum and free liquidity calculated after those obligations. For individual disbursements, OneX also processes the administration's intake-open/intake-closed decision, its reason and reference to the manually published Telegram notice, request acceptance order, amounts reserved in the dedicated wallet and any manual-reconciliation decisions. Amounts are reconciled from recharge batches, unknown-transfer records, seller reserve lots and refund records while avoiding double counting of the same liability. If a Cold Wallet address is configured, the server may transfer 90% of free liquidity when it reaches the configured threshold (500 BSC-USD by default); no automatic transfer occurs while the address is blank or while the incoming scanner has not caught up to finalized blocks. OneX retains internal operation identifiers, transaction hashes, amounts, estimated and actual fees, confirmation statuses, reserve snapshots and reconciliation logs to prevent duplicate payments, verify obligations and maintain financial auditability. Private keys for custody wallets are not information requested from users; OneX never asks for a user's personal wallet private key.

XTU wallet externalization records. When a user requests an XTU withdrawal, OneX processes the gross amount, the fixed wallet address already registered on the account, the user-specific effective valuation price, gas and contract-fee estimates, the net amount, a unique reference, transaction hash and nonce, block and receipt information, final status and any administrative control reason. The gas estimate uses the Chainlink BNB/USD feed; actual BNB gas and the 0.1% contract fee are recorded separately in the dedicated withdrawal audit ledger, while the combined fee equivalent is reflected in transaction history. The withdrawal control is initially closed and may be opened or closed by an authorized administrator. OneX does not request a user's personal wallet private key or recovery phrase and does not perform a cryptographic ownership signature challenge for the registered address. After a withdrawal is confirmed, OneX stores its own outgoing transaction evidence and does not monitor the user's wallet balance or unrelated future wallet transactions.

Deposits and payouts depend on the blockchain network, token contract, confirmation process, available liquidity and relevant service providers. If OneX announces a global disbursement, the platform may process participant information, user-submitted requests, requested and remaining amounts, payout details, payment status and administrative-review records to administer the announced request period and any applicable bonus. The platform does not automatically debit or pay an amount for which the user has not submitted a request; an unrequested or remaining balance may be referred for individual administrative review. A public transaction record may continue to exist even after an account is closed.

To monitor deposits received by the shared treasury, OneX stores a block cursor, processed-block hashes, historical checkpoints, detected transfer events and their finality status. Each chain inclusion is identified from the transaction hash, log index and block hash; this distinguishes a re-inclusion after a chain reorganization from the abandoned inclusion. The scanner queries BEP-20 logs through primary and secondary RPC providers; an optional WebSocket subscription may speed up detection but does not replace persistent block verification. After a temporary RPC outage, the system retries from the last completed range without advancing the cursor. A chain disagreement or a change to the persisted network, token-contract or treasury identity may place the scanner in “manual_review”; new automatic credits are then suspended until an administrative reconciliation is completed. This process records RPC diagnostics, checkpoints, read-only previews, “canonical”, “re_included”, “orphaned” and “unverifiable” classifications, compensating-ledger references, administrative decisions and audit reasons. A missing receipt alone is not sufficient to classify a transfer as orphaned; unverifiable cases remain blocked. The recharge batch manager runs separately and may process previously verified and finalized batches, but it holds batches associated with orphaned or unverifiable events. Transfers predating account registration or sent from an unrecognized sender may also require administrative review. Scanner status checks and reconciliation actions are logged.

5. Optional Telegram Notifications

You can use OneX without linking Telegram. If you choose to link it, OneX stores the Telegram identifiers required to send service notifications. Those messages may include your username, transaction type, amount, reference and status. Telegram processes those messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Transactional Telegram messages sent by the current service are in English. You can stop receiving them by unlinking or blocking the OneX bot, subject to the bot's available controls.

6. Sharing of Information

OneX does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, including to database hosting, email delivery, blockchain infrastructure and optional Telegram providers; to the person or team responsible for identity verification; or where disclosure is required to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users, investigate suspected fraud, enforce the Terms and Conditions or protect the security and rights of OneX.

Service providers may process information only as needed to perform the services for which they are engaged, subject to their own terms and applicable obligations. Third-party services are not controlled by OneX and may have separate privacy practices.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and operational information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, secure accounts, reconcile transactions, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record and whether a transaction is still pending.

An account that has never successfully signed in may be automatically deleted from seven days after signup. Cleanup excludes accounts administratively deactivated and accounts with a balance, transaction, deposit, payout, seller reserve or financial record that must be retained; those accounts may be held for administrative review. Deactivation reasons and account-access decision history are retained for as long as needed for security, audit and dispute handling.

You may request account deletion through the application. Deletion is subject to outstanding-balance, recharge, refund, disbursement and seller-reserve checks. Under the current account-deletion process, an account can be deleted only when the remaining balance is no more than 2 XTU and there are no pending financial or seller operations. When deletion is completed, personal account records, the user balance and the individual price state are removed to the extent permitted by retention requirements. The public BSC wallet address remains in a permanent non-reassignment registry with its historical account association; it is never made available to another user. Transaction histories, blockchain identifiers, unknown-transfer events and reconciliation records may also be retained where necessary for accounting, security or dispute resolution. Copies already sent to email providers or verification recipients are subject to their respective retention practices.

8. Security

When a new device is authorized using the password and account seed, OneX replaces the registered device, revokes the previous session token and records a security event. A notification is queued for delivery to the account email address and, when Telegram is linked, to the associated Telegram chat. Email notifications remain in a technical outbox until delivery or the retry limit is reached. Anti-replay nonces expire and are automatically deleted; device registration and replacement events are retained for account-security auditing.

OneX uses safeguards intended to protect personal and financial information, including encrypted storage of the identity-document number, cryptographic hashing of passwords and recovery seeds, access controls and transaction checks. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. OneX therefore cannot guarantee absolute security. You must keep your password and recovery seed confidential and must never send them, or a wallet private key, to someone claiming to be OneX support.

9. Children

The OneX services are intended for adults aged 18 or older and are not designed for children. OneX does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal information to OneX, contact us through the in-app Contact OneX feature so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

Some information may need to be retained or cannot be removed, including public blockchain records and transaction records required for reconciliation, security or legal purposes.
