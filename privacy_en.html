OneX Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how OneX collects, uses, stores and discloses information when you create an account or use the OneX application and services. It should be read together with the OneX Terms and Conditions.

1. Information We Process

Identity and account information. When you register, we request your full name, date of birth, residential address, declared nationality, declared country of residence, identity-document type (national identity card or passport), identity-document number, email address, account username, password and recovery seed. We also require an image of the identity document for account verification; an additional image may be provided where applicable. The identity-document number is encrypted in the application database. Passwords and recovery seeds are stored as cryptographic hashes rather than as readable credentials. If you live in the country of your nationality, you may submit a national identity card issued by that country; a passport may be used where a national identity card is not available or where it is the identity document you use. If you live in a country other than the country of your nationality, you must submit a valid passport as a foreign resident. This rule applies to all nationalities and does not single out Madagascar or any other country.

Financial and transaction information. To operate the service, we process account balances and valuation information, transaction fees, recharge and transfer records, seller-program activity, and individual and global disbursement requests addressed to ONEX. For a global disbursement, this may include whether an account was included in the announced process, whether the user submitted a request, the amount requested, any remaining balance, payment and bonus records, the request deadline, transaction status and any administrative-review or resolution reference. A participant or review record does not itself authorize OneX to debit or pay an amount that the user has not requested. We also retain relevant refund records, dates, statuses and references to maintain accounts, process transactions, prevent duplicate payments and resolve disputes.

Blockchain and wallet information. Each account may be assigned an individual public BNB Smart Chain deposit address. We also process the BSC-USD transfer details needed to identify and reconcile deposits, including the sending address, amount, transaction hash, log index, block information and timestamps. If you request a payout, we collect the public wallet address that you provide. OneX checks its address format but does not verify that you own or control that wallet. We do not ask you to provide a wallet private key.

Support and communication information. If you contact OneX, we process your message and the account and email details needed to respond. If you choose to link Telegram, we store the Telegram user and chat identifiers needed to send account and transaction notifications. Linking Telegram is optional.

Connection and security information. During registration, the server may look up your IP address for approximate country-level geolocation as a technical test. The result, including the country code MG (Madagascar), is not used to determine your nationality, select the identity document you must provide, approve or reject registration, or impose a country-of-residence restriction. The identity-document rule uses the nationality and country of residence that you declare. It does not use the IP geolocation result to infer either value or to select your document. This lookup does not verify your residence. The service also processes security events such as failed authentication attempts, password changes, recovery requests and rate-limit events to protect accounts and prevent abuse.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information described above to:

  • create, identify and manage accounts;
  • verify account information and identity documents;
  • authenticate users, support account recovery and protect credentials;
  • maintain account balances and valuations, apply disclosed fees and administer account-level service conditions;
  • identify blockchain deposits and process eligible recharges, P2P transfers, seller payments, user-submitted individual and global disbursement requests, refunds and related administrative reviews;
  • administer a global disbursement notice, request window, user-submitted requests, payment status, unrequested or remaining balances requiring administrative review, and any applicable bonus settlement;
  • send necessary account, security and transaction notifications when a communication channel has been enabled;
  • answer support requests, investigate errors, prevent fraud and maintain the reliability of the service; and
  • meet applicable legal requirements and protect the rights, users and operational security of OneX.

3. Identity Verification and Email Providers

Information and identity-document images submitted during registration may be sent by email to the person or team responsible for reviewing account registrations. Email service providers may process and store those messages and attachments as part of delivering the verification process. Do not submit identity documents belonging to another person unless you are legally authorized to do so.

OneX also uses email delivery for account-related messages, including registration, account recovery and support communications. The email provider processes the information required to deliver those messages.

4. Blockchain Processing and Public Information

OneX currently supports Binance-Peg BSC-USD transfers using the BEP-20 token standard on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56). In OneX's operating model, BSC-USD is the stablecoin payment and settlement asset associated with the platform's reserve model; it may also be referred to operationally as USDT BEP-20. XTU is not itself this blockchain token. The supported Binance-Peg BSC-USD token is a pegged representation and is not USDt directly issued by Tether. This distinction, and the risks associated with token arrangements, reserves, liquidity and blockchain operation, are described further in the Terms and Conditions. Blockchain transactions are recorded on a public network. Wallet addresses, transaction amounts, transaction hashes and related on-chain information may therefore be visible to the public and cannot be made private or deleted by OneX. Blockchain data may also be processed through the BNB Smart Chain RPC providers used to read or submit transactions.

Deposits and payouts depend on the blockchain network, token contract, confirmation process, available liquidity and relevant service providers. If OneX announces a global disbursement, the platform may process participant information, user-submitted requests, requested and remaining amounts, payout details, payment status and administrative-review records to administer the announced request period and any applicable bonus. The platform does not automatically debit or pay an amount for which the user has not submitted a request; an unrequested or remaining balance may be referred for individual administrative review. A public transaction record may continue to exist even after an account is closed.

5. Optional Telegram Notifications

You can use OneX without linking Telegram. If you choose to link it, OneX stores the Telegram identifiers required to send service notifications. Those messages may include your username, transaction type, amount, reference and status. Telegram processes those messages under its own terms and privacy policy. Transactional Telegram messages sent by the current service are in English. You can stop receiving them by unlinking or blocking the OneX bot, subject to the bot's available controls.

6. Sharing of Information

OneX does not sell your personal information. Information may be disclosed only as reasonably necessary to operate the service, including to database hosting, email delivery, blockchain infrastructure and optional Telegram providers; to the person or team responsible for identity verification; or where disclosure is required to comply with law, respond to valid legal requests, protect users, investigate suspected fraud, enforce the Terms and Conditions or protect the security and rights of OneX.

Service providers may process information only as needed to perform the services for which they are engaged, subject to their own terms and applicable obligations. Third-party services are not controlled by OneX and may have separate privacy practices.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain account and operational information for as long as it is needed to provide the service, secure accounts, reconcile transactions, resolve disputes and meet applicable obligations. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record and whether a transaction is still pending.

You may request account deletion through the application. Deletion is subject to outstanding-balance, recharge, refund, disbursement and seller-reserve checks. Under the current account-deletion process, an account can be deleted only when the remaining balance is no more than 2 XTU and there are no pending financial or seller operations. When deletion is completed, personal account records, the user balance and the individual price state are removed. The deposit-address record is retained in a disabled state, and its payout address is cleared so that the deposit address is not assigned to another account. Transaction and blockchain-reconciliation records may also be retained where necessary for accounting, security or dispute resolution. Copies already sent to email providers or verification recipients are subject to their respective retention practices.

8. Security

OneX uses safeguards intended to protect personal and financial information, including encrypted storage of the identity-document number, cryptographic hashing of passwords and recovery seeds, access controls and transaction checks. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure. OneX therefore cannot guarantee absolute security. You must keep your password and recovery seed confidential and must never send them, or a wallet private key, to someone claiming to be OneX support.

9. Children

The OneX services are intended for adults aged 18 or older and are not designed for children. OneX does not knowingly seek to collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe that a person under 18 has provided personal information to OneX, contact us through the in-app Contact OneX feature so that we can review the matter and take appropriate action.

10. Your Requests and Contact

You may contact OneX through the Contact OneX feature in the application to ask questions about this Policy or request access to, correction of, or deletion of information associated with your account. Some information may need to be retained or cannot be removed, including public blockchain records and transaction records required for reconciliation, security or legal purposes.

11. Changes to This Policy

OneX may update this Privacy Policy when the service, its processing activities or applicable requirements change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated effective date. You should review this page periodically.
