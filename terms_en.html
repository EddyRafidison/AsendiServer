3. What XTU Means

XTU (Extended Unit) is OneX's single public valuation and transfer unit. Account principal is maintained in initial assets. The public/global XTU price is common to the platform, while each account has an individual recognized price determined by its remuneration cycle and the 300 XTU account ceiling. A user's internal XTU balance is the user's initial-asset principal multiplied by that effective individual price. Therefore, two users with the same initial-asset principal may display different internal XTU values. P2P transfers remain denominated in public XTU; the sender's individual price converts the requested amount into initial assets.

Separately, OneX issues the XTU blockchain token as a BEP-20-compatible token on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56), with 18 decimals and a fixed total supply of 100,000,000,000 XTU minted once to the dedicated XTU distribution wallet. The contract exposes no subsequent mint function. The OneX accounting model uses a fixed parity of 1 XTU to 1 BSC-USD for XTU wallet externalization and reserve accounting; this accounting convention is not a price oracle, legal-tender status, a bank deposit, a guarantee of redemption or a claim against the BSC-USD issuer. Binance-Peg BSC-USD is a pegged representation and is not USDt directly issued by Tether. OneX does not guarantee profit, return, capital protection, token liquidity or the ability to redeem every amount. Users may lose some or all of the value they commit to the service.

4. Individual Remuneration and Price

Each account has an individual remuneration cycle with a base price A, a remuneration rate C and a target price B. The platform's global XTU price remains common, but the recognized individual price for an account follows global price progression only until B or the 300 XTU individual ceiling is reached. For an eligible operation group, P is the initial-asset principal immediately before the first operation in that group, V is the cumulative eligible volume and tfees is the volume-weighted fee rate actually applied. The target rate is C = 3 × tfees × min(1, 2 × V / P), and B = A × (1 + C / 100). C cannot exceed 3 × tfees. For purchases, V is the net initial assets actually credited; for sales, V is the gross initial assets debited. Purchases and sales are grouped separately. Multiple operations of the same direction may be combined; a new cycle starts when the group reaches at least 5% of P. A group below 5% does not start a cycle. The first credited acquisition for a new account initializes its principal and first cycle without dividing by zero.

The new cycle uses the post-operation global XTU price as A. While the account is actively earning remuneration, its recognized individual price follows the global price until B or until its individual valuation reaches 300 XTU. The first limit reached stops further remuneration growth: the status becomes “paused” when B is reached, or “capped” when the 300 XTU ceiling is reached first. The individual price is then frozen until a qualifying new cycle starts. An account already above 300 XTU is not repriced downward retroactively; its recognized individual price is frozen. Purchases are unavailable while the account is capped. Eligible sale volume remains grouped until the projected valuation at the current global price falls below 300 XTU and a sale group of at least 5% of its reference principal can start a new cycle. Purchases or recharges that would exceed the ceiling are rejected or remain pending until sufficient capacity becomes available.

An English-language notification is queued when B or the 300 XTU ceiling is reached. Partial operations of the same direction are grouped until the 5% minimum is met. Welcome bonuses, administrative adjustments, seller-campaign activations and uncredited deposits do not count as eligible purchase or sale volume. An individual sale to ONEX counts as an eligible sale only after the BSC-USD payment is confirmed. These rules describe an internal valuation method and do not guarantee profit, yield, liquidity or repayment.

5. P2P Transfers and Transaction Fees

When P2P transfers are enabled, you may transfer public XTU to another active OneX account. The current minimum P2P transfer is 1 XTU. The sender's requested gross XTU amount is converted into initial assets using the sender's effective individual price. The recipient bears the full applicable transaction fee (tfees), deducted from the gross amount before the net initial assets are credited. The recipient's account is then valued using the recipient's own effective individual price; the displayed XTU value of the transferred initial assets may therefore differ from the sender's gross XTU amount. The fee is retained by OneX. Its initial-asset amount is added to common backing, and its XTU reserve value is calculated using the public/global price immediately before the transfer. P2P transfers do not incur a BSC network gas charge because they are internal account entries.

Transfers are subject to account status, available balance, seller-reserve locks, the maximum account balance and any temporary system restrictions. A completed internal transfer may not be reversible. Check the recipient username and amount before confirming.

XTU blockchain transfers and wallet externalization

Every on-chain XTU token transfer is subject to a mandatory 0.1% contract fee, including transfers initiated through transfer or transferFrom. The contract retains this fee in the dedicated XTU distribution wallet; it is not the internal P2P tfees and does not represent a second charge on an internal account transfer. For a wallet externalization request, the minimum gross amount is 1 XTU. Requests are accepted only while the administration has opened XTU withdrawals and are sent only to the fixed BNB Smart Chain wallet address registered on the account. The user cannot supply a different destination in the withdrawal request. OneX validates address syntax but does not perform a cryptographic ownership challenge; the user is responsible for registering and maintaining an address they control. OneX never requests a personal wallet private key or recovery phrase.

The BNB gas estimate is converted to a BSC-USD/XTU equivalent using the Chainlink BNB/USD feed and is deducted from the requested gross amount before the token contract applies its 0.1% transfer fee to the remaining transfer amount. The actual BNB gas cost is recorded after the transaction receipt is finalized. If the estimated network cost exceeds 0.4 BSC-USD, the transfer is not signed or broadcast and is deferred for reassessment. The displayed net amount may therefore differ from an estimate. A withdrawal is treated as completed only after the XTU transfer is confirmed on-chain; an uncertain transaction is reconciled by its persisted transaction hash and nonce to prevent duplicate payments. After a confirmed transfer, OneX records the outgoing payment and does not monitor the user's personal wallet balance or unrelated transactions in that wallet.

The passage of the planned date does not activate the global mode automatically: an authorized administrator must explicitly activate it.

Normal service operations remain available during the notice period. From the moment the administration activates global disbursement, new recharges and user-to-user P2P transfers are suspended. The primary request period lasts at least 90 days; it may be configured for a longer period but never for less than 90 days. When the primary period ends, an extension of at least 30 days begins. Requests remain open during the extension, but new requests submitted during the extension do not qualify for the global bonus. Each user must personally submit a disbursement request addressed to ONEX before the extension closes. The minimum gross request during either open period is 1 XTU. A user may submit another request against a remaining balance after a previous request has been settled, provided the global request window, including the extension, remains open and all checks are satisfied. OneX does not create a payment request on a user's behalf and does not debit or pay an unrequested balance merely because global disbursement has been activated.

At activation, each participant receives an individual settlement-price snapshot calculated from that participant's individual remuneration state and current public/global price. The run-level global price is retained only as a common reference; it is not the settlement price for every participant. The participant's individual price is fixed for that participant's accepted global requests. Recipient-paid tfees are deducted from each gross request, and the estimated BSC network fee is deducted separately from the remaining BSC-USD payout. The initial assets returned through tfees are added to common backing, while their XTU reserve value is calculated at the public/global price immediately before settlement. A global bonus may be added to the net XTU amount requested, using the bonus rate announced for that global run; the rate cannot exceed 10%. A request accepted during the primary period retains its bonus even if payment is executed during the extension. New requests submitted during the extension do not receive a global bonus. For example, a 200 XTU gross request at a 3% tfees rate leaves 194 XTU after the fee; a 3% bonus on that net amount is 5.82 BSC-USD at a 1 XTU participant snapshot price, before BSC network-cost recovery. The BSC network cost is deducted separately from the payment, based on the estimated cost at submission and reconciled against the actual blockchain receipt. The bonus is funded from the administrative reserve dedicated to that purpose and remains subject to reserve and settlement liquidity available for the announced run. The global bonus does not apply to ordinary individual disbursements.

When the announced request deadline expires, no new global requests are accepted. Requests already accepted continue to be processed. A user who did not submit a request, or who still has a remaining balance after their submitted requests are settled, is placed in an administrative-review process. No automatic payment or debit is made for the unrequested or remaining balance; its handling requires an individual administrative resolution. A global disbursement or bonus does not guarantee reimbursement of all expenses a user may have incurred while using OneX. 10. Refunds, Network Costs and Payment Risks

OneX uses separate BNB Smart Chain wallets for distinct operational purposes. The Hot Wallet receives incoming recharges and transfers and acts as the operating intermediary for seller payments and refunds, including potential refunds of transfers whose sender address could not be automatically matched to an account. Uncredited recharges, reserved refunds, unknown incoming transfers held for review, seller payables and signed or submitted but unconfirmed Cold Wallet transfers are recorded as obligations and are not free funds. Free liquidity is calculated by subtracting those obligations and the configured operating minimum from the Hot Wallet's on-chain BSC-USD balance. If a Cold Wallet address is configured and free liquidity reaches at least 500 BSC-USD (a configurable threshold), the server may automatically transfer 90% of that free amount to the Cold Wallet (a configurable percentage). The remaining 10% of free liquidity stays in the Hot Wallet, in addition to protected obligations and the operating minimum. If the Cold Wallet address is blank, automatic sweeps are disabled. The server also checks incoming-scanner health, BNB gas availability and the blockchain transaction; a scanner that is behind or an uncertain state prevents a sweep. A third, separate Disbursement Wallet funds individual and global disbursements from its own BSC-USD reserve; that reserve is not included in the Hot Wallet's free-liquidity calculation. The Hot Wallet remains an operating intermediary and may transfer to the Cold Wallet only excess liquidity calculated after protecting all obligations and the operating minimum. Internal transfers between OneX wallets do not create an additional credit or debit to a user's XTU balance.
