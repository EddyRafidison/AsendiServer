3. What XTU Means

XTU (Extended Unit) is OneX's single public valuation and transfer unit. Account principal is maintained in initial assets. The public/global XTU price is common to the platform, while each account has an individual recognized price determined by its remuneration cycle and the 300 XTU account ceiling. A user's internal XTU balance is the user's initial-asset principal multiplied by that effective individual price. Therefore, two users with the same initial-asset principal may display different internal XTU values. P2P transfers remain denominated in public XTU; the sender's individual price converts the requested amount into initial assets.

Separately, OneX issues the XTU blockchain token as a BEP-20-compatible token on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56), with 18 decimals and a fixed total supply of 100,000,000,000 XTU minted once to the OneX main Hot Wallet. The XTU distribution wallet is configured separately to receive contract fees and designated consolidations; it does not receive the initial supply. The contract exposes no subsequent mint function. The OneX accounting model uses a fixed parity of 1 XTU to 1 BSC-USD for XTU wallet externalization and reserve accounting; this accounting convention is not a price oracle, legal-tender status, a bank deposit, a guarantee of redemption or a claim against the BSC-USD issuer. Binance-Peg BSC-USD is a pegged representation and is not USDt directly issued by Tether. OneX does not guarantee profit, return, capital protection, token liquidity or the ability to redeem every amount. Users may lose some or all of the value they commit to the service.

4. Individual Remuneration and Price

Each account has an individual remuneration cycle with a base price A, a remuneration rate C and a target price B. The platform's global XTU price remains common, but the recognized individual price for an account follows global price progression only until B or the 300 XTU individual ceiling is reached. For an eligible operation group, P is the initial-asset principal immediately before the first operation in that group, V is the cumulative eligible volume and tfees is the volume-weighted fee rate actually applied. The target rate is C = 3 × tfees × min(1, 2 × V / P), and B = A × (1 + C / 100). C cannot exceed 3 × tfees. For purchases, V is the net initial assets actually credited; for sales, V is the gross initial assets debited. Purchases and sales are grouped separately. Multiple operations of the same direction may be combined; a new cycle starts when the group reaches at least 5% of P. A group below 5% does not start a cycle. The first credited acquisition for a new account initializes its principal and first cycle without dividing by zero.

The new cycle uses the post-operation global XTU price as A. While the account is actively earning remuneration, its recognized individual price follows the global price until B or until its individual valuation reaches 300 XTU. The first limit reached stops further remuneration growth: the status becomes “paused” when B is reached, or “capped” when the 300 XTU ceiling is reached first. The individual price is then frozen until a qualifying new cycle starts. An account already above 300 XTU is not repriced downward retroactively; its recognized individual price is frozen. Purchases are unavailable while the account is capped. Eligible sale volume remains grouped until the projected valuation at the current global price falls below 300 XTU and a sale group of at least 5% of its reference principal can start a new cycle. Purchases or deposits that would exceed the ceiling are rejected or remain pending until sufficient capacity becomes available.

An English-language notification is queued when B or the 300 XTU ceiling is reached. Partial operations of the same direction are grouped until the 5% minimum is met. Welcome bonuses, administrative adjustments, seller-campaign activations and uncredited deposits do not count as eligible purchase or sale volume. An individual sale to ONEX counts as an eligible sale only after the BSC-USD payment is confirmed. These rules describe an internal valuation method and do not guarantee profit, yield, liquidity or repayment.

5. P2P Transfers and Transaction Fees

When P2P transfers are enabled, you may transfer public XTU to another active OneX account. The current minimum P2P transfer is 1 XTU. The sender's requested gross XTU amount is converted into initial assets using the sender's effective individual price. The recipient bears the full applicable transaction fee (tfees), deducted from the gross amount before the net initial assets are credited. The recipient's account is then valued using the recipient's own effective individual price; the displayed XTU value of the transferred initial assets may therefore differ from the sender's gross XTU amount. The fee is retained by OneX. Its initial-asset amount is added to common backing, and its XTU reserve value is calculated using the public/global price immediately before the transfer. P2P transfers do not incur a BSC network gas charge because they are internal account entries.

Transfers are subject to account status, available balance, seller-reserve locks, the maximum account balance and any temporary system restrictions. A completed internal transfer may not be reversible. Check the recipient username and amount before confirming.

XTU blockchain transfers and wallet externalization

XTU transfers on BNB Smart Chain have no blockchain transfer commission: the contract applies a 0% fee, and the transferred amount equals the amount represented by the blockchain transfer event. This rule applies to both transfer and transferFrom. It is separate from internal P2P tfees. The gross minimum for a personal-wallet withdrawal remains subject to the service configuration. Requests are accepted only when the administration has opened XTU withdrawals and are sent exclusively to the fixed BNB Smart Chain address registered to the account. The user cannot supply another destination in the request. OneX checks address syntax and does not perform a cryptographic ownership challenge; users must therefore register and retain an address they control. OneX never asks for a private key or recovery phrase.

The estimated BNB gas cost is converted to a BSC-USD/XTU equivalent using the Chainlink BNB/USD feed and is deducted from the gross requested amount before the net transfer is made. The XTU contract transfer commission is 0%. The actual BNB network cost is recorded after the transaction receipt is finalized. If the estimated network cost exceeds 0.4 BSC-USD, the transaction is neither signed nor broadcast and is deferred for reassessment. The net amount may therefore differ from the estimate. A request is considered executed only after the XTU transfer is confirmed on-chain; an uncertain transaction is reconciled using its persisted hash and nonce to prevent duplicate payment. After confirmation, OneX records the outgoing payment and does not monitor the user's personal-wallet balance or other transactions in that wallet. The withdrawal reserves BSC-USD coverage equivalent to the net XTU externalized, without transferring or consuming that physical reserve. This coverage is excluded from free liquidity and is released only after XTU actually returns to the system through a confirmed deposit. A withdrawal does not reset, restart or replace the individual remuneration cycle; the current cycle continues on the remaining balance under its unchanged parameters.

6. BSC-USD and XTU Deposits

OneX accepts only the exact configured official BEP-20 contracts for Binance-Peg BSC-USD and XTU on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (chain ID 56). Binance-Peg BSC-USD is not USDt directly issued by Tether. A token name, symbol or logo does not establish its identity; only the exact contract configured by OneX is accepted. Do not send any other token or use another network. A transfer involving a counterfeit token, another BEP-20 contract or another network may be ignored and may be unrecoverable.

BSC-USD and XTU deposits are separate operations. They can never be combined to meet the minimum or maximum. Both tokens follow the same receipt, sender-identification, minimum, credit-tranche, funding, account-capacity, expiry, retry and suspension rules. The only deposit-processing differences specific to XTU are its contract fee and its internal consolidation from the Hot Wallet to the XTU Distribution Wallet. Deposits must be sent to the official OneX receiving address displayed in the application. You must send directly from the BNB Smart Chain address registered to your account. A transfer from an exchange, pooled wallet, relayer or other unregistered intermediary address may not be attributed automatically.

Every detected, verified and finalized transfer immediately triggers a new deposit-dossier eligibility check; there is no 60-minute collection or waiting window. For a given user and token, one collection dossier below the minimum may receive subsequent finalized transfers of that same token. Its fixed expiry is calculated from the first finalized transfer and cannot be extended by later payments. Once the cumulative amount reaches at least 20 units of that token, collection closes and credit processing starts immediately when the other conditions are met. If the minimum is not reached, or part of a deposit remains blocked, the uncredited amount is tracked until the fixed 30-day expiry. A new transfer received after a dossier is closed opens a new dossier with its own expiry.

The 100-unit limit applies to each automatic credit tranche, not to the total amount a user may send. A 150-unit transfer may therefore be recorded: an initial tranche of up to 100 may be credited as soon as eligible, while the remainder stays in the same dossier pending account capacity, reserves and funding rules. Each tranche is processed only once. OneX re-evaluates pending dossiers in FIFO order after each user-balance change and through periodic recovery workers. An account's maximum individual valuation is 300 XTU; a credit that would exceed this limit remains pending, but only until the dossier's immutable 30-day expiry.

After each finalized transfer that leaves an amount pending in a deposit dossier, OneX attempts to queue an idempotent Telegram notification stating the token, received and cumulative amounts, remaining amount, exact reason, any minimum still required or capacity to free, the applicable deadline and remaining time. Deposit-dossier expiry rules are not a retention deadline for refunds. When a BSC-USD purchase dossier has not reached 20 gross BSC-USD by its fifth event, at the immutable 30-day deadline measured from its first finalized event, all source principal actually received but not credited is routed to automatic refund category D, unless a higher-priority category applies. That principal is not allocated to common reserves merely because this deposit deadline has elapsed. Automatic refunds have their own one-unit net minimum and are retained indefinitely while below that minimum.

There is no weekly ceiling on incoming transfer amounts. The seven-day rule applies only to the right to receive a purchase positively funded from OneX system reserves: a user may receive no more than one such purchase in any rolling seven-day period, regardless of its amount. This rule is shared across BSC-USD and XTU deposits. Any positive OneX reserve contribution consumes the allowance once for the relevant purchase dossier; later tranches of the same dossier do not consume it again. A purchase funded exclusively from active and available seller-campaign reserves does not consume this allowance. A mixed OneX-and-seller-funded purchase consumes it when any positive portion is funded by OneX. Sellers voluntarily activate their participation; OneX does not lock seller principal without consent. Available reserves are checked at batch closure and immediately before each credit, in the priority order specified by the service rules.

For XTU deposits, the contract applies no blockchain transfer commission: the gross amount transferred is the net amount received by the Hot Wallet. This is separate from OneX deposit fees and BNB gas. Outside a global suspension, the OneX XTU deposit fee is 75% of the normal tfees rate and is charged only once on amounts actually credited; no OneX deposit fee is charged on an uncredited remainder that expires. Consolidation gas is estimated and provisioned before credit, then reconciled against the actual consolidation receipt. XTU user credit is independent of the internal consolidation threshold. XTU received by the Hot Wallet is identified and protected in a lot register; transfers to the Distribution Wallet occur in FIFO lots of at least 100 XTU actually received net by the Hot Wallet. Even after a dossier expires, a lot below 100 XTU remains protected and accounted for in the Hot Wallet until the threshold is reached. Consolidation does not update common counters a second time.

From the effective cutoff of a global disbursement, BSC-USD and XTU deposits are suspended under the same rule. Transfers received after that cutoff are recorded but do not create a new credit or obligation that bypasses the initial snapshot. Obligations already acquired continue to be reconciled through their separate registers. The accounting parity of 1 XTU = 1 BSC-USD is not an automatic conversion between tokens and is not proof of physical BSC-USD liquidity. Before transferring, check the BNB Smart Chain network, exact token contract, official receiving address, registered sender address and amount.

Deposit monitoring and blockchain reconciliation: a deposit is processed only after the BEP-20 event, transaction receipt and block finality are verified. WebSocket is only a wake-up signal; the HTTP scanner and blockchain evidence establish receipt. After a temporary RPC outage, the scanner retries from its last completed range. If the cursor no longer matches the canonical chain or the persisted network, token-contract or treasury identity changes, the scanner enters “manual_review” and suspends new automatic credits. Ambiguous or unverifiable cases remain held; an unfinalized transfer is never credited.

Normal service operations remain available during the notice period. From the moment the administration activates global disbursement, new BSC-USD and XTU deposits and user-to-user P2P transfers are suspended under the same cutoff. Finalized transfers received after the cutoff are recorded and placed in the required control state, but they do not create new credits or obligations that change the initial snapshot. Obligations and settlements already acquired before the cutoff continue in their dedicated registers and are neither cancelled nor reused for another operation. The primary request period lasts at least 90 days; it may be configured for a longer period but never for less than 90 days. When the primary period ends, an extension of at least 30 days begins. Requests remain open during the extension, but new requests submitted during the extension do not qualify for the global bonus. Each user must personally submit a disbursement request addressed to ONEX before the extension closes. The minimum gross request during either open period is 1 XTU. A user may submit another request against a remaining balance after a previous request has been settled, provided the global request window, including the extension, remains open and all checks are satisfied. OneX does not create a payment request on a user's behalf and does not debit or pay an unrequested balance merely because global disbursement has been activated.

At activation, each participant receives an individual settlement-price snapshot calculated from that participant's individual remuneration state and current public/global price. The run-level global price is retained only as a common reference; it is not the settlement price for every participant. The participant's individual price is fixed for that participant's accepted global requests. Recipient-paid tfees are deducted from each gross request, and the estimated BSC network fee is deducted separately from the remaining BSC-USD payout. The initial assets returned through tfees are added to common backing, while their XTU reserve value is calculated at the public/global price immediately before settlement. A global bonus may be added to the net XTU amount requested, using the bonus rate announced for that global run; the rate cannot exceed 10%. A request accepted during the primary period retains its bonus even if payment is executed during the extension. New requests submitted during the extension do not receive a global bonus. For example, a 200 XTU gross request at a 3% tfees rate leaves 194 XTU after the fee; a 3% bonus on that net amount is 5.82 BSC-USD at a 1 XTU participant snapshot price, before BSC network-cost recovery. The BSC network cost is deducted separately from the payment, based on the estimated cost at submission and reconciled against the actual blockchain receipt. The bonus is funded from the administrative reserve dedicated to that purpose and remains subject to reserve and settlement liquidity available for the announced run. The global bonus does not apply to ordinary individual disbursements.

When the announced request deadline expires, no new global requests are accepted. Requests already accepted continue to be processed. A user who did not submit a request, or who still has a remaining balance after their submitted requests are settled, is placed in an administrative-review process. No automatic payment or debit is made for the unrequested or remaining balance; its handling requires an individual administrative resolution. A global disbursement or bonus does not guarantee reimbursement of all expenses a user may have incurred while using OneX. 10. Refunds, Network Costs and Payment Risks

OneX uses separate BNB Smart Chain wallets for distinct operational purposes. The Hot Wallet receives incoming deposits and transfers and acts as the operating intermediary for seller payments and refunds, including potential refunds of transfers whose sender address could not be automatically matched to an account. Uncredited deposits, reserved refunds, unknown incoming transfers held for review, seller payables and signed or submitted but unconfirmed Cold Wallet transfers are recorded as obligations and are not free funds. Free liquidity is calculated by subtracting those obligations and the configured operating minimum from the Hot Wallet's on-chain BSC-USD balance. If a Cold Wallet address is configured and free liquidity reaches at least 500 BSC-USD (a configurable threshold), the server may automatically transfer 90% of that free amount to the Cold Wallet (a configurable percentage). The remaining 10% of free liquidity stays in the Hot Wallet, in addition to protected obligations and the operating minimum. If the Cold Wallet address is blank, automatic sweeps are disabled. The server also checks incoming-scanner health, BNB gas availability and the blockchain transaction; a scanner that is behind or an uncertain state prevents a sweep. A third, separate Disbursement Wallet funds individual and global disbursements from its own BSC-USD reserve; that reserve is not included in the Hot Wallet's free-liquidity calculation. The Hot Wallet remains an operating intermediary and may transfer to the Cold Wallet only excess liquidity calculated after protecting all obligations and the operating minimum. Internal transfers between OneX wallets do not create an additional credit or debit to a user's XTU balance. Wallet outages, liquidity shortages, BNB fees, network errors or security incidents may nevertheless delay or prevent an operation.

Automatic refunds cover only transfers of the exact official BSC-USD or XTU contracts received by the corresponding OneX receiving wallet on BNB Smart Chain Mainnet (chain ID 56) and reconciled after finality. The engine assigns each source event to exactly one category: A, an official transfer from an unknown address; B, BSC-USD received during the global-disbursement period, including the 30-day claim extension; C, uncredited principal and new official transfers from a deactivated account; or D, uncredited BSC-USD principal in an active account's purchase dossier that failed to reach 20 gross BSC-USD within at most five events by its immutable 30-day deadline. Category D never includes an amount already credited and does not allocate funds to common reserves. A 1% retention is calculated for each source event on the amount actually received. For a compatible grouped payout, estimated gas is deducted once for the single outgoing transaction. A refund is eligible when its net amount reaches at least one unit of the source token; BSC-USD and XTU are never mixed. Each event must be finalized and must have passed its own minimum 30-minute delay. If the net remains below one unit, funds are retained and reassessed without any expiry or age-based review deadline. Estimated gas must not exceed the operational ceiling of 0.4 BSC-USD. The automatic refund is sent to the same source wallet. No user request, EIP-191/EIP-1271 signature or prior administrative approval is required for the normal route. A genuine security or reconciliation anomaly may place a case in manual_review until a documented resolution. An address with an outstanding refund obligation cannot be used for a new signup.
