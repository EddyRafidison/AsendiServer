3. What XTU Means

XTU (Extended Unit) is the public valuation unit used by OneX to display the value of an account's initial assets. It is not a separate user balance stored in the database and it is not the BSC-USD token. User balances and internal reserve balances are recorded in initial assets. The current global XTU price is calculated as follows:

Global XTU price = common.total_units_price / common.backed_units

Your displayed XTU balance is your initial-asset balance multiplied by the current global price. The global price is common to all accounts and may change as a result of platform accounting operations. A historical price is locked for certain transactions and seller or disbursement obligations; a later price change does not revalue a transaction that has already been accepted with a price snapshot.

XTU is an internal platform valuation unit. In the OneX economic model, it is supported by the platform's reserve and settlement framework, which uses Binance-Peg BSC-USD on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20, referred to operationally as USDT BEP-20) as its external payment and settlement asset. XTU is not itself BSC-USD, legal tender or a bank deposit. This backing model does not constitute a guarantee that each XTU can always be converted or redeemed at a fixed 1:1 rate for BSC-USD, nor does it create a direct claim against the token issuer. The supported Binance-Peg BSC-USD token is a pegged representation and is not USDt directly issued by Tether. OneX does not guarantee that the global XTU price will rise, remain stable or reach a particular value. No profit, return, capital protection or redemption amount is guaranteed. The ability to complete a recharge, transfer, seller payment or disbursement depends on the applicable rules, enabled services, available reserves, treasury liquidity and blockchain operation. Users may lose some or all of the value they commit to the service.

4. Individual Price Threshold

OneX maintains an individual reference price for each account while applying one global price to all accounts. The individual target is calculated using the reference price A and the transaction-fee rate actually applied to the most recent acquisition:

B = A × (1 + 2 × tfees / 100)

A successful recharge or purchase/sale resets the reference price A using the global price resulting from that operation. An incoming P2P transfer also resets the recipient's reference basis after the system checks whether the recipient had already reached a threshold before the transfer. The applied fee rate used for the target is retained according to the individual price-cycle rules. This mechanism does not create a separate bonus balance and does not guarantee an automatic credit or payment.

When the global price reaches or exceeds B, the individual accumulation cycle is paused and a threshold event is recorded. A later fall in the global price does not automatically cancel a threshold that has already been recorded. At the threshold, OneX records the account's principal balance S0. The pause remains until successful outgoing P2P sales reach at least 50% of S0. Only outgoing P2P transfers count toward this requirement. Receiving a P2P transfer, activating a priority-seller campaign, using a seller-reserve lot, or making an individual disbursement to OneX does not count.

A recharge made while the account is paused may update A and B, but it does not remove the pause or replace the 50% sale requirement. Once the required outgoing P2P sales have been completed, a new individual price cycle starts using the then-current global price. If Telegram is linked, OneX may send a notification when the threshold is reached; Telegram is optional and is not required to use the service.

5. P2P Transfers and Transaction Fees

When P2P transfers are enabled, you may transfer XTU to another active OneX account. The current minimum P2P transfer is 1 XTU. The sender is debited the gross amount requested. The recipient bears the full applicable transaction fee (tfees), so the recipient receives the net amount after that fee is deducted. The applicable rate is the rate configured and disclosed by the application for the transaction. The fee is retained by OneX. P2P transfers do not incur a BSC network gas charge because they are internal account entries.

Transfers are subject to account status, available balance, seller-reserve locks, the maximum account balance and any temporary system restrictions. A completed internal transfer may not be reversible. Check the recipient username and amount before confirming.

6. Recharges and Supported Blockchain Payments

When recharge services are enabled, OneX accepts the currently supported payment asset only: Binance-Peg BSC-USD using BEP-20 on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56), referred to operationally as USDT BEP-20. This token is not USDt directly issued by Tether. Do not send another token or use another network. A deposit sent to an incorrect address, token contract or network may be unrecoverable.

Each account is assigned an individual public deposit address. Finalized on-chain transfers are identified and grouped into recharge batches over a 60-minute period. The automatic recharge minimum is 12 BSC-USD per batch. The net amount credited is calculated after the applicable tfees and the configured recovery of eligible BSC costs paid by OneX. Deposits are credited using the global price captured for the relevant operation, subject to the account and reserve rules.

The 140 BSC-USD maximum and seven-day cooldown apply only to the part of a recharge funded from OneX reserves. Amounts funded from active seller reserves are exempt from that ONEX-funded limit. The total resulting account balance may not exceed 300 XTU. Recharge credit may be delayed or held when minimums, limits, reserve availability, account status, confirmation requirements or support checks are not satisfied. A batch may remain pending until it becomes eligible for processing or is otherwise resolved.

Recharge funding follows this priority: (1) OneX's inactive initial-asset reserve; (2) active seller-reserve lots, consumed in FIFO order; and (3) the distributable portion of backed initial assets above the remaining protected administrative reserve. Pending disbursement reservations reduce the capacity available for system-funded recharges. A user's own locked seller-reserve lot is not used to fund that same user's recharge.

7. Priority-Seller Participation

An account may become eligible for priority-seller participation only after its individual price threshold has been reached and its main balance is at least 10 XTU at the current global price. When the user activates a campaign, 50% of the principal balance is committed automatically to the seller reserve. The user does not choose a separate lock amount. The committed amount remains blocked through the associated seller-reserve lot and cannot be transferred as ordinary available balance.

A price cycle can be used to activate only one seller campaign. A campaign normally lasts seven days. Seller-reserve lots are consumed in FIFO order when they are used to fund eligible recharges for other users. Amounts consumed create seller payout obligations valued at the global price locked when the campaign was activated. Seller payouts are grouped into batches of up to 300 BSC-USD gross. No tfees are charged on seller payouts, but the estimated BSC gas recovery may be deducted; actual network gas is recorded separately. A cancellation request does not immediately release unused locked reserve. Unused amounts are released at normal expiry or after an administrative unlock, while amounts already consumed remain payable obligations.

Activating as a priority seller does not count as an outgoing P2P sale for the 50% requirement described in Section 4.

8. Individual Disbursement to OneX

An individual disbursement is a user-requested sale to OneX. To submit a request, the account must have a public balance of at least 290 XTU, must have a valid BNB Smart Chain payout address registered, must not have an active seller campaign or unresolved disbursement, and must satisfy the applicable amount and remaining-balance settings. The default minimum requested amount is 10 XTU, but the platform configuration may change that minimum. A confirmed individual disbursement is subject to a rolling 30-day cooldown before another request can be accepted.

The user enters a gross XTU amount. OneX converts it to initial assets using the global price locked when the request is accepted and applies the configured tfees. The expected BSC-USD payout and price snapshot are recorded with the request and are not changed by later global price movements. The payout is submitted by the OneX treasury after processing and is considered complete only when the relevant blockchain transaction is confirmed. The individual-disbursement mode does not deduct a separate BSC gas recovery from the user's payout under the current implementation.

9. Global Disbursement

Global disbursement is a separate liquidation mode initiated only by an explicit, authenticated and recorded OneX administrative decision. It is not triggered automatically by a price movement or by an individual user request. When a global run is started, OneX records a single global price snapshot for the run and processes eligible accounts in batches. Global disbursement does not apply tfees. The configured BSC gas recovery is deducted separately from each applicable payout.

Before a global run begins, outstanding individual disbursements, seller campaigns and reserves, seller payouts, recharge batches and refunds must be resolved. During an active run, certain account and stock operations are suspended to protect the settlement process. An account with a missing or invalid payout address, or one requiring reconciliation, may be placed in manual review without its balance being debited. Payments remain subject to treasury BSC-USD and BNB liquidity, network availability and blockchain confirmation. A global run is not complete until its accepted payments are finalized or the necessary manual reconciliation has been completed.
