3. What XTU Means

XTU (Extended Unit) is the public valuation unit used by OneX to display the value of an account's internal assets. It is not a separate blockchain token and is not the BSC-USD token. Account and platform balances are maintained under OneX's internal accounting rules. The displayed XTU value may change as the platform's global valuation changes. A price or amount recorded for an accepted transaction may be fixed for that transaction and will not necessarily change when later valuations change.

XTU is an internal platform valuation unit supported by OneX's reserve and settlement framework. The external payment and settlement asset currently supported is Binance-Peg BSC-USD on BNB Smart Chain using BEP-20, referred to operationally as USDT BEP-20. XTU is not itself BSC-USD, legal tender or a bank deposit. The use of BSC-USD as a settlement asset does not guarantee that every XTU can always be converted or redeemed at a fixed 1:1 rate, and it does not create a direct claim against the token issuer. Binance-Peg BSC-USD is a pegged representation and is not USDt directly issued by Tether. OneX does not guarantee that the XTU valuation will rise, remain stable or reach a particular value, and does not guarantee profit, return, capital protection or redemption of every amount. Users may lose some or all of the value they commit to the service.

4. Individual Price Threshold

OneX may apply an individual accumulation cycle to an account based on the platform's global valuation and the applicable transaction-fee rate. The individual target is calculated as B = A × (1 + 3 × tfees / 100), where A is the reference price used for the cycle and tfees is the fee rate applicable to the last acquisition taken into account. Therefore, when tfees is 3%, target B represents a 9% increase over A. When the account-specific target is reached, the accumulation cycle is paused under the service rules. Targets for active cycles may be recalculated when a new rule is applied; cycles already paused retain their trigger history.

During a pause, the account may be required to complete qualifying outgoing P2P sales equal to at least 50% of the principal balance recorded for that cycle before a new accumulation cycle can begin. Receiving a P2P transfer, activating a priority-seller campaign or making an individual sale to OneX does not count toward that outgoing-sale requirement. A recharge made during the pause does not by itself remove the pause. OneX's internal valuation calculations and operational thresholds may be adjusted to maintain the integrity of the service; such changes do not create a guaranteed return or a separate bonus entitlement.

5. P2P Transfers and Transaction Fees

When P2P transfers are enabled, you may transfer XTU to another active OneX account. The current minimum P2P transfer is 1 XTU. The sender is debited the gross amount requested. The recipient bears the full applicable transaction fee (tfees), so the recipient receives the net amount after that fee is deducted. The applicable rate is the rate configured and disclosed by the application for the transaction. The fee is retained by OneX. P2P transfers do not incur a BSC network gas charge because they are internal account entries.

Transfers are subject to account status, available balance, seller-reserve locks, the maximum account balance and any temporary system restrictions. A completed internal transfer may not be reversible. Check the recipient username and amount before confirming.

6. Recharges and Supported Blockchain Payments

When recharge services are enabled, OneX accepts the currently supported payment asset only: Binance-Peg BSC-USD using BEP-20 on BNB Smart Chain (chain ID 56), referred to operationally as USDT BEP-20. This token is not USDt directly issued by Tether. Do not send another token or use another network. A deposit sent to an incorrect address, token contract or network may be unrecoverable.

All recharges are sent directly to OneX's single shared treasury address, which is displayed in the application while the service is available; no individual deposit address is generated. To identify a user, OneX compares the from address in a finalized BEP-20 Transfer event with the fixed wallet address registered at signup. Automatic credit also requires the block timestamp to be later than the account registration time; earlier transfers or transfers whose timing cannot be established with certainty are retained for administrative review. You must therefore send funds directly from that registered wallet. A withdrawal from an exchange, pooled wallet, relayer or other intermediary may expose a different sender address; it will not be automatically attributed and may be recorded as an unknown transfer for administrative review. Recognized and finalized transfers are grouped into a 60-minute recharge session. The total amount in an automatically processed session must be at least 20 BSC-USD and must not exceed 100 BSC-USD. A session outside this range may be held for review instead of being credited automatically. The maximum public account balance is 300 XTU. Before an outgoing BSC-USD payment, refund or disbursement, OneX estimates BNB Smart Chain fees and converts the estimated cost into BSC-USD using the Chainlink BNB/USD price feed. If the estimated cost exceeds 0.4 BSC-USD, the transaction remains queued without being signed or broadcast and is re-evaluated later. This estimate does not guarantee the final on-chain cost. The credited amount is determined after applicable transaction fees; because deposits are received directly by the shared treasury, no intermediate-address consolidation cost is allocated to recharge sessions. A seven-day restriction may apply to the portion funded from OneX system reserves; seller-funded portions are subject to their own availability and conditions. The user's own sending fee charged by their wallet or provider is not a OneX recharge fee.

Recharge credit may be delayed or held when account eligibility, session limits, timing limits, reserve availability, blockchain confirmation requirements, a support review or another platform control is not satisfied.
